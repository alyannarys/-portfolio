My story

A little about me

Since 2017 International clients Remote teams

Since graduating in 2017, I’ve built my career around design, content, and project coordination — starting as a graphic and web designer and eventually moving into roles that blend creativity with marketing and strategic project leadership.

Over the years, I’ve worked with international clients and teams, creating visual content, managing projects, supporting marketing campaigns, maintaining websites, and helping keep day-to-day operations organized.

Today, I bring all of those skills together to help businesses with both the creative work and the behind-the-scenes tasks that keep things moving.
